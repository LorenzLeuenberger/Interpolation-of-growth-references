# Original LMS values of growth references
This raw data folder contains LMS values from growth references. The original LMS values remain the intellectual property of their respective creators and are subject to their original licensing terms. This repository is intended for research and educational purposes.

## Sempé

The mean and standard deviation of the Sempé growth references were published in 1979:
Sempé, Pédron, and Roy-Pernot. Auxologie: méthode et séquences. Laboratoire Théraplix, 1979.

## Prader
The mean, standard deviation, an empirical centiles of the Prader growth references were published in 1989:
Prader, et al. Physical growth of Swiss children from birth to 20 years of age. First Zurich longitudinal study of growth and development. Helv Paediatr Acta Suppl, 1989. 52: p. 1-125.

## WHO
The LMS values of the WHO child growth standards are available online: [https://www.who.int/tools/child-growth-standards](https://www.who.int/tools/child-growth-standards)
The LMS values of the WHO growth references for school-aged children and adolescents are available online: [https://www.who.int/tools/growth-reference-data-for-5to19-years](https://www.who.int/tools/growth-reference-data-for-5to19-years)

## UK-WHO
The LMS values of the UK-WHO growth references are available online: [https://growth.rcpch.ac.uk/clinician/growth-references/](https://growth.rcpch.ac.uk/clinician/growth-references/)

## Braegger
The WHO growth references were re-analyzed for Switzerland in 2011 [Braegger et al.](https://www.paediatrieschweiz.ch/neue-wachstumskurven-fur-die-schweiz/).
The LMS values were kindly provided by the Swiss Society of Pediatrics--pädiatrie schweiz. These data ownership of these LMS values remains with pädiatrie schweiz together with their original licensing terms.

## Eiholzer
Alternative growth references for Swiss children were published in [2019](https://doi.org/10.1080/03014460.2019.1677774) and [2025](https://doi.org/10.3390/jcm14165912).

## Hermanussen
Heuristic L values for weight and BMI were published by Hermanussen et al. in [2012](https://dx.doi.org/10.1111/j.1651-2227.2011.02415.x.), and in the book Hermanussen. Auxology - Studying Human Growth and Development. E. Schweizerbart'sche Verlagsbuchhandlung (Nägele u. Obermiller), 2013. ISBN: 978-3-510-65278-5.
